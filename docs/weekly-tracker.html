What counts as one serving

Vegetables — 1 cup raw / ½ cup cooked. Potatoes don't count.
Sugary drinks — 8 oz soda, juice, sweet coffee, energy drink.
Whole grains — ½ cup cooked oats/quinoa/brown rice, 1 slice 100% whole-wheat.
Red / processed meat — 3 oz beef/pork/lamb; deli meat, bacon, and sausage count double.
Fruit — 1 medium piece or ½ cup berries. Whole fruit, not juice.
Ultra-processed — packaged snacks, fast food, sweetened cereal, frozen meals.
Legumes / soy — ½ cup beans or lentils, 4 oz tofu, 1 cup edamame.
Fatty fish — 3.5 oz salmon, sardines, mackerel, herring, or trout.
Healthy fat — 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil or ½ avocado.
Protein — target is bodyweight × 0.7 g (220 lb → 154 g).
Nuts / seeds — 1 oz, about a small handful.
Reading the sheet: green circles are servings to fill in — a full row of green is that component maxed. Red squares are tallies for things to leave empty; each mark you make costs points. Dashed fish circles only need two filled across the whole week. The score is a rolling 7-day window, so a thin day is diluted by the six around it — no daily reset, no starting over.

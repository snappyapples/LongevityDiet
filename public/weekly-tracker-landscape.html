One serving =

Vegetables — 1 cup raw / ½ cup cooked (not potatoes)
Legumes / soy — ½ cup beans or lentils, 4 oz tofu
Whole grains — ½ cup oats / quinoa / brown rice
Healthy fat — 1 tbsp olive oil or ½ avocado
Fruit — 1 medium piece or ½ cup berries (not juice)
Nuts / seeds — 1 oz, a small handful
Fatty fish — 3.5 oz salmon, sardines, mackerel

10 g of protein =

1½ eggs · ⅓ cup Greek yogurt · 1¼ oz chicken · 1½ oz salmon · 4 oz tofu · ½ cup lentils · 1 cup milk · ½ scoop whey

How to use it

The seven green rows are 70 of the 100 points — a full grid is a perfect week on the add side.

Fish: two dashed circles, any two days.

Protein sits outside the 100. Target = bodyweight × 0.7 g (220 lb → 154 g ≈ 15 circles). Resets daily — a miss can’t be made up later.

The other 30 points come from what you leave out; that side isn’t on this sheet on purpose.
